Uploaded letters & alternative templates

Uploaded wording replaces the standard letter for this draft. A separate super administrator still approves and issues each letter. Keep the document’s own letterhead, or add the employing company’s letterhead.

Up to 10 MB. PDF: up to 30 flattened pages; no automatic text replacement. Word: embedded images only, no macros or external links.

How to add automatic fields in Word

Type a placeholder in your Word file, for example {{employee_name}}. Upload the file to fill it from the employee record. Text without placeholders stays as written. Additional placeholders become editable fields.

Examples: Dear {{employee_name}}, your revised basic salary is RM {{revised_basic}} from {{effective_date}}.

Use {{basic_increment_pct}} for the increment percentage. Enter % beside the placeholder in Word. Fields are text only; formulas and loops are not supported.

Review the source and its placeholders before publishing. This does not approve or issue any employee letter.
Company must match the employee and uploaded template. Existing letterheads are retained; choose “keep” if your document already includes one. PDF content is fitted below an added letterhead; Word content flows down.
